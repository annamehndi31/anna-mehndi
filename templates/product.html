📖 How To Use?

{% if product.category == "Wall Art & CO2 Laser" %}
  1. Unbox carefully and remove all protective packaging.
  2. Choose your desired location on the wall.
  3. Use a level to ensure straight placement.
  4. Mark drill points or use adhesive strips provided.
  5. Mount the piece securely on the wall.
  6. Step back and enjoy your beautiful wall art! 🖼️
{% elif product.category == "Marbles & Bangles" %}
  1. Handle with care — delicate item.
  2. Clean with soft dry cloth only.
  3. Store in provided packaging when not in use.
  4. Avoid contact with water and chemicals.
  5. Enjoy your beautiful accessory! 💎
{% elif product.category == "Mehndi Cones" %}
  1. Cut the tip of the cone to desired thickness.
  2. Apply henna in desired pattern on skin.
  3. Let dry for 2-4 hours for best results.
  4. Scrape off dried henna gently.
  5. Avoid water for 12 hours after application.
  6. Enjoy your beautiful mehndi! 🌿
{% else %}
  1. Wash and dry the skin.
  2. Carefully remove white backing paper from stencil.
  3. You can cut the stencil into small parts to apply on your skin.
  4. Apply stencil to skin, adhesive side down.
  5. Slowly peel back remove protective sheet.
  6. Apply any henna to skin showing through the stencil.
  7. Let dry according to paint instructions.
  8. Once henna is completely dry, rub off excess paint and remove stencil.
  9. And you're done! 🌿
{% endif %}
